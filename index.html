About Us

Your Trusted Partner in Technology Solutions

Silverline Techno Solutions is a leading technology solutions company specializing in CCTV Surveillance & Security Solutions, GIS Mapping, Land Surveying, Drone Technologies, and Fire & Safety Systems.

We provide comprehensive, end-to-end solutions that help government organizations, public sector enterprises, educational institutions, industrial facilities, infrastructure projects, and private organizations enhance security, improve operational efficiency, and support digital transformation initiatives.

Our expertise spans the entire project lifecycle—from consultation, design, supply, installation, integration, testing, commissioning, training, and long-term maintenance support.
